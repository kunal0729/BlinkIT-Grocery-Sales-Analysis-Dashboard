# BlinkIT Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyze BlinkIT grocery sales performance across products, outlet types, outlet locations, and establishment years.

## Tools Used

- Power BI
- Power Query
- DAX
- Microsoft Excel

## Key KPIs

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | ₹1.20M |
| Total Items | 8,523 |
| Average Sales | ₹141 |
| Average Rating | 3.97 |

## Key Insights

- Fruits & Vegetables generated the highest sales at approximately ₹178K, followed by Snack Foods at ₹175K.
- Supermarket Type 1 was the highest-performing outlet type, generating approximately ₹788K in sales.
- Tier 3 outlets generated the highest sales at approximately ₹472K.
- Outlets established in 2018 recorded the highest sales at approximately ₹205K.
- Medium-sized outlets generated the highest overall sales at approximately ₹508K.
- Low-fat products contributed approximately ₹717K in sales, significantly higher than regular-fat products.

## Business Takeaways

- Focus on high-performing categories such as Fruits & Vegetables and Snack Foods.
- Explore further growth opportunities in Tier 3 markets.
- Investigate the factors contributing to the strong performance of outlets established in 2018.
- Analyze Supermarket Type 1 to identify the factors driving its higher sales performance.
